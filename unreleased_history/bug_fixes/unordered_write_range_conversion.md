Fixed a bug where `min_tombstones_for_range_conversion` could permanently delete a key written with `DBOptions::unordered_write`. A scan could run after the write's sequence number was published but before the key was in the memtable, and the converted range tombstone then covered the key for every read at the scan's sequence number or higher, also after the key reached the memtable. Read-path range tombstone conversion is now disabled when `unordered_write` is true.
