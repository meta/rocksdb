Fixed a bug where an iterator with `ReadOptions::prefix_same_as_start` combined with `ReadOptions::total_order_seek` or `ReadOptions::auto_prefix_mode`, including every `prefix_same_as_start` iterator under `DBOptions::prefix_seek_opt_in_only`, could skip keys within the seek prefix after changing direction (`Prev()` then `Next()`, or the reverse) when prefix filters are used. `prefix_same_as_start` now takes precedence over `total_order_seek` and `auto_prefix_mode`. Also fixed `CoalescingIterator` and `AttributeGroupIterator` keeping an error status after a later `Seek*()`, which left them invalid; every `Seek*()` now clears earlier errors, as documented for `Iterator`.
