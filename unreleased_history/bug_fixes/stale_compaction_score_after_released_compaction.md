Fixed a bug where writes could stop forever after a compaction released its input files without finishing, for example a manual compaction canceled by `DisableManualCompaction()` or `CompactRangeOptions::canceled`, or a compaction aborted by `AbortAllCompactions()` or `AbortCompactions()`. The column family kept a compaction score computed without those files, so no compaction was scheduled for it. When writes were already stopped by too many L0 files, no flush ran to fix the score. The score is now recomputed and the column family is queued for compaction again. Aborted compactions are queued when compactions are resumed.
