Fixed a bug with user-defined timestamps and a merge operator where a flush or compaction could combine a merge operand at or above `full_history_ts_low` with an older version of the same key below `full_history_ts_low`. A read at a timestamp between the two versions could then return a wrong value or NotFound. For example, with `full_history_ts_low` 5, a Merge at timestamp 6 could absorb a Put at timestamp 3, and a read at timestamp 5 no longer saw the Put. The older version is now kept as a separate entry.
