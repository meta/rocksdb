Fixed a heap-use-after-free in coroutine reads (`CoroDB::CoGet()`, `CoroDB::CoGetEntity()`, `CoroDB::CoMultiGet()`, `DB::GetAsync()` and `DB::MultiGetAsync()`): work started during the read could capture its `folly::RequestContext` and restore it after the read returned, and the restore then called the DB's `Env`, which may already have been destroyed with the DB. A restore on the read's own thread after the read could also replace that thread's perf context and disable stats collection. Restores after the read ends are now ignored, and restores never call the `Env`.
