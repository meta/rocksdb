Fixed HyperClockCache with `strict_capacity_limit` lowering its usage to the capacity at the start of an insert into a shard over capacity, as `SetCapacity()` lowering the capacity leaves one, before evicting anything. An entry erased while that insert was evicting took its charge off usage that no longer included it, so usage underflowed and other inserts failed with `MemoryLimit` until the insert finished, and other inserts meanwhile could be admitted into capacity that entries still in the cache took.
