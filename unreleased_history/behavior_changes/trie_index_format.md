The experimental trie index (`TrieIndexFactory`) has a new, incompatible on-disk format. SSTs with older trie blocks are read through the standard index in `kStandardDefault` until a compaction rewrites them, and fail to open in `kStandardRequired`, `kCustomDefault` and `kCustomOnly`. SSTs an older release wrote in `kCustomOnly` cannot be opened at all, so rewrite them in another mode before upgrading. Older releases cannot read the new blocks.
