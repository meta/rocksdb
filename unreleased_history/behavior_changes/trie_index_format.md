The experimental trie index (`TrieIndexFactory`) has a new on-disk format that is smaller, faster to search, and supports data blocks past 4 GiB. It cannot read old trie blocks: such SSTs fall back to the standard index in `kStandardDefault` and fail to open in modes that require the trie, so compact in `kStandardDefault` first. Earlier releases cannot open new SSTs while the factory is configured. `DB::Open()` and `CreateColumnFamily()` now reject the trie with a non-bytewise comparator, except in `kStandardOnly`.
