With blob direct write, every WAL sync (`SyncWAL()`, `FlushWAL(true)`, a write with `sync=true`, and the sync of closed WALs before a flush) first syncs the blob direct write files and the directory entries of new blob files, so it takes longer. `SyncWAL()` and `FlushWAL(true)` do not keep writers out, and the sync does not cover the blob value of a record written while they run: after a power loss, a read of that key can fail.
