With `DBOptions::prefix_seek_opt_in_only`, a `ReadOptions::prefix_same_as_start` iterator now returns `Status::NotSupported` from `SeekToFirst()` and `SeekToLast()`, with or without iterator bounds, and `Status::InvalidArgument` from `Seek()` and `SeekForPrev()` with a target outside the prefix extractor's domain. Use `Seek()` or `SeekForPrev()` to a key in the wanted prefix instead. Without `prefix_seek_opt_in_only`, such scans keep their results, but a `prefix_same_as_start` iterator no longer creates read-path range tombstones (`min_tombstones_for_range_conversion`) from scans started by `SeekToFirst()` without `iterate_lower_bound`, `SeekToLast()` without `iterate_upper_bound`, or an out-of-domain seek target, because they can skip live keys.
